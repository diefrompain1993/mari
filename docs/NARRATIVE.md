# Narrative production notes
Working title: «Проявление». Alternatives considered: «Контур» (too static), «Ближе» (less specific to visual grammar).

User amendments take precedence over the master spec: emotional events may be reordered or transformed into other circumstances; no literal calendar reconstruction. User will sit beside the player; final question remains open in person, with no answer buttons or implied scripted acceptance. Stay is an exceptionally meaningful shared song; placement remains a later collaborative decision. Use an original non-imitative temporary cue until a user-supplied recording is available. Both characters initiate conversations. Humor ranges from meta-irony to straightforward silliness. «14 времени» originated in the girl's younger sister using that phrase for 2 pm; never explain this origin in the game.

Arcs: notice → approach → learn → stay → care → understand → say. Girl's visible details represent knowing her, hero's details represent recognizing his own emotions. Neither person is saved or made whole by the other.

The recurring architectural line connects spaces. Pixelization enters through a close examination of the hand, not an apparent loss of self. Care is reciprocal and does not exhaust the girl. Walking together happens before the question; after it, only breathing, wind and light.
